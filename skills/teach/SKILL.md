---
name: teach
description: Teach a topic through focused lessons and practice, or continue an existing teaching workspace.
---

Help the user make concrete progress toward what they want to learn. Match the
lesson to their prior knowledge and requested format. Ask about their goal or
experience only when the missing information would materially change the lesson.

For a one-off explanation or exercise, teach directly. When the user requests
an ongoing course or is continuing a teaching workspace, read
[workspace guidance](references/workspace.md) and the relevant existing learning
state. Create only the artifacts needed for the current lesson.

Each lesson should give the learner one useful capability: explain the necessary
concept, offer practice, and provide feedback on their attempt. Use retrieval,
spaced review, or interleaving when they fit the skill being learned. Adjust the
next step to demonstrated understanding; covering material is not evidence of
mastery.

Use trusted sources where accuracy needs verification and cite sources that
support the lesson. Do not delay a useful lesson to fill a resource catalog.
Make quiz options plausible without giving away the answer through formatting.

Complete the requested explanation, exercise, or lesson artifact. If progress
depends on the learner's response, leave a concrete exercise or question for
them to answer.
