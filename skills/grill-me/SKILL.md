---
name: grill-me
description: Stress-test a plan, decision, or idea through an interview when the user asks to be grilled or challenged.
---

Interview the user to expose consequential assumptions, tradeoffs, and failure
modes. Aim for a decision they can defend and a clear account of what remains
uncertain.

Use the conversation and relevant local evidence to establish what is already
known. Investigate facts you can look up; ask the user for judgments, preferences,
and information you cannot access.

Ask a manageable round of independent questions, prioritizing decisions that
could change the plan. Give a recommendation and its tradeoff when you have
enough evidence. Wait for answers before asking questions that depend on them.
Adapt subsequent rounds to the answers instead of following a fixed checklist.

Finish when the consequential decisions are settled, remaining uncertainties
have an owner or next step, or the user asks to stop. Summarize the resulting
plan, tradeoffs, and open questions. An interview alone does not authorize
implementation; continue into implementation when the user has requested it.
